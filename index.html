Zack D Style — Protein

Mechanism film · Protein

What actually happens when a muscle fibre tears.

Lifting doesn't build muscle. It damages it. Growth happens during the repair, and the repair can only use the material you give it — so the film goes inside the arm. Fibres split open under load, then protein and branched-chain amino acids flood the tear and stitch it back together.

Built in the explainer format currently dominating supplement advertising, the one people actually watch to the end. Produced start to finish without a camera.

The Match

0:15 · 9:16
Product film · Creatine

A match that burns out, and lights again.

Creatine isn't a stimulant and it doesn't give you a bigger tank — it recharges the one you have. So the ad is a single match: it fires, it burns down in ten seconds, it dies to carbon. Then it lights again.

No people, no stock footage, no borrowed clips. One object, lit once, doing the one thing the product actually does.
